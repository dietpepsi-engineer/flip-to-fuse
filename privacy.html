Flip to Fuse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Flip to Fuse is a puzzle game made by gunjunhani. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. Flip to Fuse does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few settings on your own device only: your best score, whether sound is on, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third parties

We do not share information with anyone, because we do not have any. Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information about downloads under their own privacy policies.

Children

Flip to Fuse is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
