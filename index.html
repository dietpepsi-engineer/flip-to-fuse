Flip to Fuse

A calm puzzle of two-sided tiles. Roll, flip, match colors, and keep your combo going.

How to play

Swipe to roll every tile one cell. Each tile that moves flips over and shows its other color. When two tiles with the same top color meet, they fuse into one. Fuse on every swipe to grow your combo.
